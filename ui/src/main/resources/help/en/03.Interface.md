# Interface

Xeres offers the following features, accessible from the tabs at the top.

- [Home](03a): share your Xeres ID and add friends
- [Contacts](03b): manage all contacts available in your network
- [Chat](03c): discuss in real time within chat rooms
- [Forums](03d): participate in synced discussions
- [Channels](03e): publish files, news or blog posts in a channel for your subscribers
- [Boards](03f): share pictures and links in boards
- [Files](03g): search, send and receive files anonymously

## Status bar

In the status bar down below, the number of connections to your friends is displayed, as well as other indicators.

### Link

This LED displays the quality of your link.

### DHT

The DHT LED only appears if the DHT is enabled (see [Settings / Network](04c))
