# Link Preview

## Details

The link preview is generated on your side, that is, Xeres will connect to the third party site described by the link and fetch the data from it. The third party site will see an incoming connection from you, even if you didn't click on the link. Special care has been taken to use a "generic" user-agent to avoid making
Xeres detection too easy.

## Risks

🟠 High

While the third party website will have difficulties detecting that you're using Xeres, they can use it to build a profile related to your IP address, depending on what URLs your friends are giving to you.
