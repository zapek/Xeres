# External URL Confirmation

## Details

Disabling this feature will directly open external URLs by the application configured in your OS (usually your web browser).

## Risks

🟠 High

Xeres does take some steps to try to signal external URLs (with a small link icon) and the tooltip that you get when hovering over the URL.

It will also detect malicious URLs (unencrypted protocols and fakes links), show them in red and warn about them.

Nevertheless, someone on a public chat could try to, for example, expose your IP address by making you click on a link on his own server, which is why you should be careful when disabling that feature.
